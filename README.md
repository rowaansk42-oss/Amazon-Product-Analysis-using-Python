# 📊 Amazon Product Analysis using Python

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Amazon product data using Python. The objective is to understand product pricing, discounts, customer ratings, and category-wise performance through data cleaning, visualization, and business insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Clean and preprocess raw Amazon product data.
* Analyze product categories and pricing.
* Explore customer ratings and review trends.
* Identify highly rated and most reviewed products.
* Generate business insights through visualizations.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

---

## 📂 Dataset Information

* Dataset: Amazon Product Dataset
* Total Records: 1,399 (after data cleaning)
* Features Analyzed:

  * Product Name
  * Category
  * Discounted Price
  * Actual Price
  * Discount Percentage
  * Rating
  * Rating Count

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

* Removed unnecessary columns.
* Handled missing values.
* Removed duplicate records.
* Converted price columns to numeric format.
* Converted discount percentage to numeric values.
* Created a new `main_category` column.

---

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis

The project includes:

* Product Distribution by Category (Bar Chart)
* Product Distribution by Category (Pie Chart)
* Rating Distribution
* Top 10 Highest Rated Products
* Top 10 Most Reviewed Products
* Average Rating by Category
* Average Discount by Category
* Discount Range Distribution (Pie Chart)
* Actual Price vs Discounted Price (Scatter Plot)
* Correlation Heatmap

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* Electronics contain the highest number of products.
* Most products have ratings between 4.0 and 4.5.
* Heavy discounts are common across categories.
* Popular products generally receive a higher number of customer reviews.
* Product price has a weak relationship with customer ratings.

---

## 📈 Business Recommendations

* Increase inventory in high-performing categories.
* Improve product quality in lower-rated categories.
* Continue offering competitive discounts.
* Encourage customers to submit product reviews.
* Promote highly rated products through targeted campaigns.
